Energy +1.6% — refiners lead as crude holds its Gulf premium, even after an early dip on Russian diesel

Data as of 3:35 PM ET.

Why it's moving

  • 🛢️ Crude recovered an early slide: WTI traded at $93.68, -0.06% at 10:24 AM ET and Brent at $105.70, -0.94%, according to TradingNews, after both fell about 2% early on news of extra Russian diesel supply.
  • 🇷🇺 Russian diesel capped the upside: Russia pledged 75,000 b/d of diesel in October, rising to 250,000 b/d by year-end, per TradingNews, after the Trump administration temporarily lifted US sanctions on Russian fuel.
  • ⚠️ Gulf risk kept a floor under prices: Saudi Arabia said Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport was shut through Saturday after weekend strikes, tanker attacks near the Strait of Hormuz continued, and Gulf refined-product exports remain at 60% of prewar levels, per TradingNews.
  • 🏭 That is a refiner setup, not just a crude setup: tight refined-product supply lifts US refiners even when crude itself goes sideways — and refiners, not services, are driving XLE today.

Driving it

Refiners are the driving sub-segment, ahead of the oil majors:

  • VLO (Valero) +4.0% — $453.23
  • MPC (Marathon Petroleum) +2.9% — $472.01
  • CVX (Chevron) +1.4% — $217.99
  • XOM (Exxon Mobil) +1.2% — $174.21

All figures per Finnhub as of 3:35 PM ET. Communication Services was the next-best sector at XLC +1.1%, while Healthcare lagged at XLV -1.3%, dragged by Johnson & Johnson.

Technicals

  • VLO +4.0% — well above the 20-day MA ($404.91) and 50-day MA ($369.96); broke the prior high near $449.77 to $455.36 today; hourly uptrend, 15-min holding near session highs after opening at $440.
  • MPC +2.9% — above the 20-day MA ($417.06) and 50-day MA ($381.82); cleared $470 resistance to a $474 session high; support at the $458.81 prior close.
  • XOM +1.2% — above the 20-day MA ($163.85) and 50-day MA ($161.90); session high $174.89 above the recent $172.55 swing high.
  • XLE +1.6% — above the 20-day MA ($63.38) and 50-day MA ($62.80); broke the recent $66.42 high to $67.15, a new session and 52-week high area.

Takeaway

Energy is not winning because crude spiked — it is winning because refined products are scarce: when diesel stays tight, US refiners capture the Gulf premium even on a flat crude day.
