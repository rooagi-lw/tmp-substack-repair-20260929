October 6 — three names worth watching on today's earnings slate. Lamb Weston reports before the open, while Constellation Brands and Penguin Solutions report after the close.

Lamb Weston (LW) — reports before the open, call at 9:00 AM ET

  • Estimates: $0.59 EPS on $1.65B revenue (fiscal Q1)
  • What to watch:
  • North America volumes — the McDonald's supplier needs restaurant-traffic recovery to repeat last quarter's beat ($0.87 vs. $0.63 expected)
  • FY2027 guidance — currently $2.95–$3.25 EPS; any move of the range resets the stock
  • Pricing power and "Focus to Win" turnaround progress; international demand trends

Constellation Brands (STZ) — reports after the close

  • Estimates: $3.55 EPS on $2.54B revenue (fiscal Q2)
  • What to watch:
  • Beer volumes — did Modelo and Corona hold share through the summer selling season? Beer is the engine; softness here is the bear case
  • Full-year outlook — estimates have drifted down for 90 days and the stock is off ~28% in six months; a reaffirmed or raised guide matters more than the beat/miss
  • Wine & spirits drag and tariff cost pressure on Mexican imports

Penguin Solutions (PENG) — reports after the close, call at 4:30 PM ET

  • Estimates: $0.77 non-GAAP EPS on ~$521M revenue (fiscal Q4)
  • What to watch:
  • AI backlog — AI-driven businesses were 74% of sales, up 104% YoY last quarter; the backlog shows whether demand keeps broadening
  • Integrated Memory — surged 111% YoY to $275M last quarter; sustainability matters more than the headline beat
  • Margins and cash — gross margin was 28.1% and operating cash flow went negative (-$75M) last quarter; watch for stabilization
  • FY26 outlook — guided to ~22% sales growth and ~$2.60 non-GAAP EPS; a raise keeps the AI trade alive
