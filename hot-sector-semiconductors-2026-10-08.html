Hot Sector: Semiconductors -4.9%

Semiconductors -4.9% — chip stocks cratered as a fresh OpenAI revenue report shook faith in AI spending, and no one wanted to catch the falling knife. (as of 3:31 ET)

Why it's moving

  • 📰 FT bombshell: the Financial Times reported, citing investor documents, that OpenAI's annualized revenue is nearing $50 billion — roughly $20 billion less than the ~$70 billion previously signaled. OpenAI declined to comment; the gap fanned doubts about AI demand and monetization (per FT via CNN, Barron's).
  • 🛢️ Brent crude surged over 5% to $105.21/bbl on Middle East and hurricane worries, feeding inflation anxiety — while the 10-year Treasury yield touched 5.35%, its highest since 2002 (per Investopedia, tradingnews).
  • 🧠 Good news got ignored — and that is information: TSMC's September revenue jumped 55% YoY and its Q3 total of NT$1.49 trillion beat consensus; Samsung guided Q3 operating profit to 107.4T won (~$80B), ahead of estimates. The stocks fell anyway.
  • ⚖️ Intel-specific overhang: questions over Elon Musk's Terafab project, where TSMC is now in talks to take a role, clouded the most visible potential outside customer for Intel's foundry (per tradingnews).

Driving it

AI/logic chips led the wreckage, with optical and memory names piling on: AMD -5.2%, Intel -5.1%, Broadcom -4.8%, Nvidia -3.8%. Also battered: TSM -5.6%, Coherent -11.2%, Celestica -9.8%, Arm -9.9%.

Technicals

  • NVDA -3.8% ($230.22) — above the 20-day MA ($226) and 50-day MA ($221); resistance $239 (3-month high); hourly downtrend from $243; 15-min opened $235 and is basing near the $230 low.
  • AMD -5.2% ($615.95) — above the 20-day MA ($593); resistance $649 (yesterday's 3-month high); hourly rolled over from $658; 15-min dumped from a $639 open to the $613 low.
  • AVGO -4.8% ($357.89) — above the 20-day MA ($355), below the 50-day MA ($372); resistance $376–380; 15-min sold off from a $371 open toward the $357 low.
  • INTC -5.1% ($106.78) — below the 20-day MA ($114), above the 50-day MA ($103); support $103–105, resistance $113–114; daily downtrend all week ($119 → $107); 15-min weak from $110 toward $105.

Takeaway

The AI trade is repricing from demand optimism to monetization skepticism — and right now, not even record earnings are saving chip stocks.
