Semiconductors -2.2% — the chip selloff is back, and this time memory is the epicenter.

Why it's moving

  • 💾 China's CXMT announced progress in advanced DRAM technology over the weekend, raising fears of new competition for Micron and Sandisk in a market they dominate, per StockTwits' Monday premarket report.
  • 🤖 Fresh questions about OpenAI's revenue outlook — reportedly near $50B annualized, not the $70B figure that circulated in September, per a media report — put AI infrastructure spending back under the microscope.
  • 📱 A media report says Apple told suppliers to cut iPhone 18 Pro orders, citing weaker demand after memory-driven price increases.
  • 📅 ASML and TSMC report earnings this week — traders are de-risking ahead of the prints, per StockTwits.

Driving it

Memory is leading the fall: Sandisk (SNDK) -5.2% and Micron (MU) -4.1% on the CXMT threat. AI/logic is caught in the crossfire: Intel (INTC) -2.6% and AMD -2.5%. Context: SOXX just posted its worst week in a month, -5% last week, per StockTwits.

Takeaway

Memory is where the AI trade's weakest link just got tested — CXMT's DRAM progress turned a valuation wobble into a supply-side scare.
