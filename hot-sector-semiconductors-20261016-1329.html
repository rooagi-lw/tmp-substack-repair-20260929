Why it's moving

  • 💰 CoreWeave (CRWV) -11.2% led the AI-infrastructure rout, according to Barron's, as investors kept fretting about AI spending and the company's debt load; its shares are down about 24% this week, and Reuters reported the yield on its October 2031 bond briefly surged past 16% on October 15.
  • ⚖️ Credit markets are flashing red: credit default swaps on CoreWeave debt hit a record this week, with swaps pricing in a more than 50% chance of default within five years, per Barchart.
  • 📉 The slide is now historic in length: SOXX is on track for a ninth consecutive daily decline, its longest streak since 2006, and KLA (KLAC) -2.9% and Lam Research (LRCX) -3.4% are on pace for a 10th straight daily fall — their longest losing streaks on record, according to Barron's.
  • 🏭 Equipment and memory led the downside: Semtech -3% after its 2030 revenue targets signaled a growth slowdown that disappointed investors, per Barron's, while memory names Micron and SanDisk fell with the group.

Driving it

AI logic chips, memory and custom silicon all traded lower together — this was a financing scare, not a single earnings miss. AMD -4.9%, INTC -4.6%, MU -3.9% and MRVL -3.9% were the biggest large-cap decliners, while NVDA -0.6% held up best. The other side of the rotation: Energy (XLE) +2.2% and Utilities (XLU) +1.2%.
