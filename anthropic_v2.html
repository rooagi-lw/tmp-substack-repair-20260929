The most important private company in AI just showed its cards — and the numbers are staggering on both sides of the ledger.

📌 What happened

All filing figures below are from Reuters' September 28–29 reporting on Anthropic's confidential draft prospectus, which is not public.

  • 📰 Reuters obtained Anthropic's 261-page confidential IPO prospectus on Monday evening — the first full look inside the Claude maker's finances.
  • 💰 The filing targets a valuation above $2 trillion — more than double the $965 billion Anthropic estimated for itself in May.
  • 📉 2025 financials: revenue $4.59 billion (+1,088% YoY), operating loss $8.06 billion, net loss $41.97 billion — though about $34 billion of that is a non-cash accounting charge, not money spent running the business.
  • 🏗️ The bombshell: $518 billion in planned cloud, compute and infrastructure commitments — the largest corporate buildout plan ever disclosed.
  • ⚠️ Nearly 80 pages warn of "existential risks," including models that could show "self-preserving behaviors" and resist shutdown.

🕰️ How we got here

  • 2021: Founded by ex-OpenAI researchers led by Dario Amodei; safety-first research becomes its brand — and its moat.
  • February 2026: Raises $30 billion at a $380 billion valuation. Three months later, a $65 billion Series H at $965 billion — overtaking OpenAI ($852 billion March raise) as the world's most valuable AI startup.
  • February–March 2026: Trump directs the government to stop working with Anthropic; the Pentagon declares it a supply-chain risk; Anthropic sues. The fight still overhangs the story.
  • April 2026: Launches Mythos, its most advanced model, built for defensive cybersecurity.
  • June 2026: Confidentially files its IPO prospectus with the SEC — edging OpenAI in the race to public markets.
  • July 2026: Settles the authors' training-data class action for $1.5 billion.
  • Meanwhile revenue went vertical: a $9 billion run rate at end-2025 → $47 billion (May) → $65 billion (July) → $100 billion+ (mid-September), per Reuters, Bloomberg and the New York Times — an 11x ramp in under a year.
  • 2026 quarterly actuals, per CNBC and the Financial Times: Q1 revenue hit $4.8 billion; Q2 was tracking $10.9 billion — more than all of 2025 in a single quarter — set to be Anthropic's first profitable quarter, with a second straight profitable quarter expected.

🔢 By the numbers

  • $2T+ IPO target vs $965B four months ago — the filing doubles its own mark.
  • $4.59B 2025 revenue against $12.65B in total operating expenses; compute spending alone tripled to $7.33B.
  • $20.28B cash on hand (Dec 31, 2025) vs $518B in future infra commitments — the IPO isn't optional, it's the funding plan.
  • ~25% of revenue comes from just two customers, many not locked into long-term contracts — concentration is the quiet risk.
  • The raise could reach $100B, among the largest IPOs ever; Nvidia is reportedly in talks for a $10B anchor stake.

📊 Why it matters

  • This is the moment AI's economics go public. Every hyperscaler, chipmaker and cloud stock reprices off this filing — $518B of planned compute spend is a direct read-through for Nvidia, Broadcom, AMD and the memory makers.
  • It sets the comp for OpenAI's eventual listing — and establishes how public markets price profitless hypergrowth at multi-trillion-dollar scale.
  • The customer-concentration disclosure will hang over every enterprise-AI revenue multiple from here.

⚖️ Bull vs. bear

  • 🐂 Bull: The fastest revenue ramp in tech history — 11x in under a year, approaching profitability, enterprise Claude adoption compounding. At ~20x the current run rate and ~10x the $190–200B 2028 revenue forecast, $2T is simply the price of the AI buildout's purest play.
  • 🐻 Bear: $518B of commitments against $20B of cash means the valuation has to fund the spending — it's circular. Two customers drive a quarter of revenue with no long-term lock-in; open-source models keep getting cheaper and better; and the filing itself warns its own technology could pose "existential" risks.

👀 What to watch next

  • The S-1 goes public in October — Q3 actuals vs the $100B run rate is the first truth test.
  • A November listing (after the midterms) targeting a $100B raise — watch how the roadshow receives $2T.
  • Whether Nvidia's $10B anchor is confirmed by name in the filing.
  • The Trump/Pentagon fight — any escalation is an overhang no multiple can ignore.

Takeaway: Anthropic is asking public markets to fund the largest infrastructure buildout in corporate history on the back of the fastest revenue ramp in tech history — $2 trillion is either the bargain of the AI age or its peak.
