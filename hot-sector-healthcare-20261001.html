Hot Sector: Healthcare -2.40%

Healthcare -2.40% — the worst sector in the S&P 500 today, and it is broad, not one stock's problem.

💊 Lilly pulls the plug on Foghorn. Eli Lilly scrapped its cancer-drug partnership with Foghorn Therapeutics after the lead candidate FHD-909 failed to show enough efficacy — Foghorn plunged 46% to $1.94, a record low, and is cutting 40% of its staff to stretch cash into late 2029, according to Barron's. Lilly itself fell 3.0%.

🏥 Managed care under pressure. UnitedHealthcare rolled out its 2027 Medicare Advantage plans today (covering 94%+ of Medicare-eligible individuals), and UNH still slid 2.8% ahead of its October 13 earnings, while Cigna fell 2.9%. A federal judge partially denied UNH's motion to dismiss a CalPERS lawsuit on September 30, keeping the legal overhang alive.

💉 Pharma-wide rotation. Johnson & Johnson -3.2%, Merck -3.4%, Amgen -2.8%, Vertex -3.2% — all red while Technology (+1.91%) and semiconductors (+1.92%) rip. That is rotation, not a fundamental scare.

Driving it: pharma and managed care, with biotech hit hardest at the margins — MRK -3.4%, JNJ -3.2%, LLY -3.0%, VRTX -3.2%, CI -2.9%, UNH -2.8%.

Technicals — LLY -3.0%: below the 20-day ($1,151.85) and 50-day ($1,177.57) moving averages; support $1,141.50 (session low), then $1,115.70 (20-day low); resistance $1,158.72 (session high), then $1,184.78. Hourly downtrend across five sessions; sold off from the $1,155 open today.

Technicals — JNJ -3.2%: below the 20-day ($268.78) and 50-day ($266.07); support $258.74 (session low), then $245.49 (3-month low); resistance $264.00. Hourly downtrend, faded all session from the $263.68 open.

Technicals — MRK -3.4%: below the 20-day ($147.37) but holding above the 50-day ($142.02); support $143.82, then $142.02; resistance $145.39. Choppy all day — basing near the 50-day.

The one thing to remember: when every sub-segment sells off together while tech rallies, the tape is rotating — watch whether Lilly holds the $1,141 area to keep this a rotation and not a breakdown.
