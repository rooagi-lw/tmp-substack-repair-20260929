🔥 Utilities +2.5% — nuclear stocks are powering the market's most rate-sensitive sector as Google signs the largest tech-to-nuclear power deal ever struck. Data as of 1:35 PM ET.

Why it's moving

  • ⚛️ Google–Constellation 20-year PPA: Google contracted 3,590 MW from Constellation — including 890 MW of new nuclear capacity via uprates at 11 reactors in Illinois, Pennsylvania and New Jersey, backed by $4.3B of Constellation investment. First power lands in 2028; upgrade work runs through 2032/33 (per Reuters, WSJ, and the companies' release). The WSJ calls it the largest-ever uprate deal between a nuclear operator and a tech company.
  • 📈 Sector sympathy: Talen Energy +13%, Vistra +12%, NRG +9% on the news (per WSJ).
  • 📉 Yield relief: the 10-year Treasury slipped to ~5.26%, down from a 24-year high above 5.34% — a bid for rate-sensitive utilities (per Investopedia and Reuters).
  • 🏦 Fed repricing: odds of an October Fed rate hike collapsed to 23% from 71% a week ago (per Reuters).
