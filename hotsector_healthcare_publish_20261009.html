Hot Sector: Healthcare +0.9%

Healthcare +0.9% — Humana's Medicare star-rating comeback is repricing the entire managed-care trade.

Why it's moving

💊 The ratings reset — Late Thursday, CMS published the 2027 Medicare Advantage star ratings, and Humana emerged as the biggest winner: its flagship contract covering 2.4 million members was restored to 4 stars, and 95% of its MA members will be in 4-star-or-better plans for 2027 — far above the 60–70% J.P. Morgan expected, per Reuters. Crossing 4 stars unlocks federal bonus payments that can be worth hundreds of millions per contract.

🚀 Humana +12.8% — HUM surged to $436.73, a new 52-week high, after jumping as much as 16% premarket. Baird upgraded to Outperform with a $596 price target (from $390), seeing more than $35 in adjusted 2028 EPS; Barclays lifted its target to $582, per The Fly.

⚖️ Winners and losers — The same scorecard punished rivals: CVS -1.2% after three contracts were cut to 3.5 stars, Alignment Healthcare -23% on its California downgrade, while Clover Health +3.4% earned a five-star PPO rating, per IBD and TradingView.

Driving it

Managed-care payers are doing all the work: HUM +12.8%, CLOV +3.4%, UNH +1.3% (despite J.P. Morgan cutting its 4-star mix estimate to 67% from 81%), and CVS -1.2%.

Technicals

  • HUM +12.8% @ $436.73 — new 52-week high, well above the 20-day ($389.53) and 50-day ($386.71) moving averages; old high $428.88 is now support. Five-day return +15.0%. Today: gapped to a $450.49 open, tagged $456.50, fading to $436.73 by midday.
  • CVS -1.2% @ $86.73 — below the 20-day ($88.96) and 50-day ($93.65) MAs, sitting near the 200-day ($87.82). Support $84.23, resistance $88.96. Five-day return +1.2%. Opened $85.90, bounced to $88.86, faded.
  • UNH +1.3% @ $375.63 — pinned to the 20-day MA ($375.77), below the 50-day ($391.01). Support $373, resistance $387.52 (today's high). Five-day return +2.3%.

Takeaway: One government scorecard just redrew the 2028 earnings map for managed care — Humana bought back its bonus payments, and the market is pricing a $35-plus EPS future.
