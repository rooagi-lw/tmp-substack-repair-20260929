Five widely followed names report today, Thursday, October 15 — four before the open and one after the close. Here is the setup for each.

TSM — Taiwan Semiconductor (before the open)

  • Consensus: EPS of $4.46 per ADR on revenue of $45.67 billion (FactSet, via Investor's Business Daily); other trackers cluster at $4.42–$4.45 on about $45.5 billion.
  • What to watch: Fourth-quarter guidance — the AI demand signal the whole chip sector trades on.
  • What to watch: Gross margin against the company's 65%–67% guided range, after 67.7% in Q2.
  • What to watch: AI / high-performance computing demand, leading-edge capacity utilization, and capital spending plans.

SCHW — Charles Schwab (before the open)

  • Consensus: EPS of $1.66 on revenue of $7.19 billion (Zacks Consensus Estimate, up 26.7% and 17.3% year over year, respectively).
  • What to watch: Net interest income and client cash sorting — whether client cash keeps moving back into higher-yielding products.
  • What to watch: Net new assets and total client assets for the pace of organic growth.
  • What to watch: Trading revenue momentum and expense discipline.

PNC — PNC Financial (before the open)

  • Consensus: EPS of $4.89 on revenue of $6.59 billion (AlphaStreet, 16 analysts); MarketBeat is close at $4.90 on $6.61 billion.
  • What to watch: Net interest income and margin — management guided sequential loan growth of 1%–2% and NII up about 3%–3.5%.
  • What to watch: Fee income, guided down roughly 5%–5.5% sequentially after a strong capital-markets quarter.
  • What to watch: Credit quality — charge-offs and provisions as loan growth continues.

PLD — Prologis (before the open)

  • Consensus: EPS of $0.78 on revenue of $2.18 billion (MarketBeat); Core FFO consensus is about $1.57–$1.58 per share, against full-year Core FFO guidance of $6.22–$6.30.
  • What to watch: Occupancy and rent growth — the cleanest read on warehouse demand.
  • What to watch: Same-store NOI and leasing volume after a record signing quarter in Q2.
  • What to watch: Any change to full-year guidance, plus progress on the data center development pipeline.

IBKR — Interactive Brokers (after the close)

  • Consensus: EPS of $0.67 on revenue of $1.86 billion (AlphaStreet and Seeking Alpha consensus; MarketBeat: $0.668 on $1.86 billion).
  • What to watch: Trading volumes (DARTs) — whether the activity surge that drove a record Q2 held through September.
  • What to watch: Net interest income on client cash and margin loans as rates shift.
  • What to watch: New account growth and total client equity.
