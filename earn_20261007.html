Earnings Preview: LEVI, APLD

Two names report after today's closing bell — denim icon Levi Strauss and AI datacenter builder Applied Digital.

Levi Strauss (LEVI) — reports after close; call 5:00 PM ET

  • Consensus: EPS $0.36, revenue ~$1.62B
  • What to watch:
  • FY 2026 EPS guidance: currently $1.46–$1.52 — does management raise it?
  • Gross margin: targeting ~61.8% — is the margin expansion story intact?
  • Consumer demand: two-thirds of last quarter's growth came from units, not price hikes — are shoppers still buying?

Applied Digital (APLD) — reports after close; call 5:00 PM ET

  • Consensus: EPS -$0.30 (loss), revenue ~$116.3M (per MarketBeat; another estimate source shows ~$137.6M — watch where the print lands)
  • What to watch:
  • Rental revenue ramp at the Polaris Forge 1 campus — the steady income stream vs. lumpy tenant fit-out payments
  • New capacity commentary: 75 MW went live October 2 (after the quarter ended August 31), plus build-out funding
  • Any new customer contracts or backlog updates
