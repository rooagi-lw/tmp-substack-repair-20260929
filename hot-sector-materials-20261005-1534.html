🔥 Fire line

Materials +2.28% — copper miners and industrial chemicals led a broad rally as a soft U.S. jobs report cooled Fed rate-hike bets and copper tightened again.

Why it's moving

  • 💼 Soft jobs data cooled the Fed. Nonfarm payrolls rose just 29,000, below all economists' estimates, per Bloomberg — traders pared bets on an October rate hike, lifting commodities and cyclicals.
  • 🟠 Copper rose for a second straight session, up 0.6% to $14,347 a ton on the LME, according to Bloomberg — rebounding after its worst week since March on weak China industrial data.
  • ⚒️ Supply squeeze is back. A strike at the Mantoverde copper-gold mine in Chile has added to disruption fears, per ING, with LME inventories low and the cash-to-3-month spread in backwardation.
  • 🥇 Gold near records (~$4,140/oz), silver above $60 — keeping a bid under gold miners like Newmont.

Driving it

Copper miners & metals led: Freeport-McMoRan (FCX) +5.77% at $73.31, Newmont (NEM) +1.74% at $116.72. Industrial gases and specialty chemicals followed: Linde (LIN) +2.75% at $482.35, Ecolab (ECL) +2.78% at $278.66.

Technicals

  • FCX +5.77% — above the 20-day ($71.82) and 50-day ($70.75) MAs; 3-month trend +20.2%; hourly uptrend, +2.7% over the last 5 sessions; opened $72.22, held into the $73.51 session high.
  • LIN +2.75% — above the 20-day ($467.64), reclaiming the 50-day ($479.95); 90-day range $457.50–$546.64; hourly steady climb; today $476.58–$485.93.
  • ECL +2.78% — above the 20-day ($274.88), knocking on the 50-day ($279.10); today's sharp bounce after days of chop — opened $272.06, closed near the $279.57 high.
  • NEM +1.74% — still below the 20-day ($121.88) and 50-day ($118.31) MAs, but 3-month trend +20.3%; rebounded from the $113.58 intraday low.

Takeaway

When copper tightens on both supply shocks and a softer dollar path, the whole materials complex re-rates — and Freeport's 5.8% surge shows the leverage cuts both ways.
