October 1 — three widely followed names report today. Nike headlines after the close, while Accenture and McCormick already posted before the open this morning.

Accenture (ACN) — reported before the open

  • Estimates: $3.18 EPS on $18.03B revenue
  • Results: Beat on both — $3.29 EPS on $18.7B revenue, with new bookings of $22.2B (vs. $19.9B expected)
  • What to watch:
  • AI demand story — Accenture stopped breaking out advanced AI bookings after Q1; investors read AI monetization through total bookings and management's language
  • FY2027 guidance: revenue growth of 3%–6% in local currency, EPS of $14.39–$14.81
  • Stock is up ~16% premarket, above its 200-day average for the first time in months after being down 31% year-to-date (according to Barron's and IBD)

McCormick (MKC) — reported before the open

  • Estimates: $0.76 EPS on $1.98B revenue
  • What to watch:
  • Gross margins — consensus implied margin compression; watch whether pricing offsets input-cost inflation
  • Volume vs. price contribution to the ~15% revenue growth (market share signal)
  • Full-year outlook reaffirmed: net sales growth of 13%–17%, adjusted EPS of $3.05–$3.13

Nike (NKE) — reports after the close

  • Consensus estimates: $0.44 EPS on $11.32B revenue (implying ~11% and ~3% year-over-year declines)
  • What to watch:
  • Greater China revenue — fell 12% last quarter; the key drag on the turnaround under CEO Elliott Hill
  • Gross margins — management projected expansion could begin this quarter after seven straight quarters of decline; tariff costs remain a wildcard
  • "Win Now" strategy progress — wholesale recovery in North America and NIKE Direct performance; the full new product cycle doesn't arrive until spring 2027, so this is a credibility test
  • Sentiment is extreme: stock down ~77% from its 2021 peak, short interest near 87M shares; Jefferies expects a beat at $0.48 EPS
