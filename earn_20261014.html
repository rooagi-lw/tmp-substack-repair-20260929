Wednesday, October 14 brings a heavy pre-open slate: chip-equipment leader ASML, three big financials — Bank of America, Morgan Stanley and BlackRock — and insurer Progressive, all reporting before the open. Here is what to watch.

ASML Holding (ASML) — before the open

  • Consensus: EPS $12.47 on revenue of $13.18 billion (Zacks Consensus Estimate; TipRanks put EPS near $12.14 on $13.16 billion)
  • What to watch: Q4 and full-year sales guidance — whether AI-driven EUV demand is strong enough to lift the outlook again
  • What to watch: gross margin, system bookings and order commentary for 2027–2028, plus any update on export restrictions

Bank of America (BAC) — before the open

  • Consensus: EPS $1.11 on revenue of $30.58 billion (AlphaStreet, 18 analysts; other previews cluster near $1.10 on about $30.6 billion)
  • What to watch: net interest income — consensus near $16.55 billion — and whether loan growth offsets margin pressure after the September rate move
  • What to watch: investment banking fees, seen down more than 10% as dealmaking cooled, versus trading strength; credit quality and expenses

Morgan Stanley (MS) — before the open

  • Consensus: EPS $2.94 on revenue of $20.19 billion (AlphaStreet, 16 analysts; TradingView/FactSet near $2.95 on $19.97 billion)
  • What to watch: equities and fixed-income trading — can trading momentum offset a cooler deal and underwriting pipeline?
  • What to watch: Wealth Management net new assets and net interest income, plus compensation and expense discipline

BlackRock (BLK) — before the open

  • Consensus: adjusted EPS $14.29 on revenue of $7.52 billion (AlphaStreet, 15 analysts; MarketBeat near $14.36 on $7.45 billion)
  • What to watch: net inflows and total AUM — consensus AUM near $15.67 trillion — and organic base-fee growth, especially in iShares ETFs and private markets
  • What to watch: performance fees, seen down about 20% year over year in previews, technology services revenue, and operating margin

Progressive (PGR) — before the open

  • Consensus: EPS $4.35 on revenue of $22.61 billion (MarketBeat / StockTwits earnings calendar; MarketBeat also lists $4.30)
  • What to watch: combined ratio and underwriting margin — catastrophe losses and loss costs versus premium growth
  • What to watch: policies in force and net premiums written growth in personal auto, and any slowdown as pricing competition returns

All five report before the opening bell, so the premarket tone for banks, asset managers, insurers and chip equipment will be set early.

Sources: AlphaStreet, Zacks, TipRanks, MarketBeat, TradingView/FactSet and Seeking Alpha earnings calendars and previews, October 2026.
