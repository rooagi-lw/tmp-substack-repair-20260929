Q3 earnings season kicks off this morning, Tuesday, October 13, with four of the biggest US banks and two healthcare heavyweights all reporting before the open. Here is what to watch.

JPMorgan Chase (JPM) — before the open

  • Consensus: EPS $5.93 on revenue of $51.23 billion (FactSet via Barron's; InsiderFinance)
  • What to watch: net interest income and whether full-year NII guidance holds; investment banking and markets revenue against a tough second-quarter comparison
  • What to watch: credit quality — provisions, charge-offs and any commentary on consumer resilience

Goldman Sachs (GS) — before the open

  • Consensus: EPS $12.86 on revenue of $16.83 billion (FactSet via Barron's; InsiderFinance)
  • What to watch: equities trading strength versus the softer FICC trading management flagged in September; investment banking fees and backlog conversion
  • What to watch: expenses — management guided non-compensation costs up more than $500 million sequentially — and Asset & Wealth Management flows

Johnson & Johnson (JNJ) — before the open

  • Consensus: EPS $2.53 on revenue of $25.38 billion (Seeking Alpha; TipRanks)
  • What to watch: Innovative Medicine — Tremfya and Darzalex growth offsetting the Stelara decline — and MedTech sales growth
  • What to watch: any update to full-year guidance, plus commentary on new drug launches and competition

UnitedHealth Group (UNH) — before the open

  • Consensus: EPS $4.14 on revenue of $111.31 billion (Seeking Alpha; MarketBeat)
  • What to watch: medical costs and the medical loss ratio, Medicare Advantage membership and Star ratings, and Optum performance
  • What to watch: full-year guidance, set at $19.50-$20.00 EPS, and any early commentary on the 2027 outlook

Citigroup (C) — before the open

  • Consensus: EPS $2.65 on revenue of $23.72 billion (MarketBeat; AlphaStreet)
  • What to watch: return on tangible common equity against the target of slightly above 11% for the full year, net interest income and markets revenue
  • What to watch: buyback pace, transformation and restructuring costs, and credit quality

All five report before the opening bell, so expect the tone for banks and healthcare to be set in the premarket.

Sources: Barron's / FactSet, Benzinga, Seeking Alpha, MarketBeat, AlphaStreet, TipRanks and InsiderFinance earnings calendars and previews, October 2026.
