Hot Sector: Energy +1.1%

Energy +1.1% — refiners are ripping while crude keeps falling.

Why it's moving

  • ⚖️ G7 nations agreed Friday to release 100 million barrels of crude and diesel from emergency reserves over the next four months — with a substantial diesel release in the first 20 days, according to the Wall Street Journal — putting a lid on fuel prices.
  • 📦 OPEC+ agreed Sunday to hold November production quotas steady, and Middle East crude exports climbed back above pre-war levels (~18.5M barrels/day, per Kpler data), easing near-term supply anxiety.
  • 🛢️ Saudi Aramco unexpectedly cut its November prices to Asia by $3 a barrel, the widest discount to the Oman/Dubai benchmark since June 2020, per Reuters — cheaper feedstock for refiners.
  • ⚠️ The Houthis claimed missile and drone strikes on Saudi Aramco sites in Riyadh and Khurais (not confirmed by Saudi Arabia). Crude barely flinched: December Brent at $102.80 (-0.2%), December WTI at $88.40 (-1.2%), per the Wall Street Journal.

Driving it

Refiners, not oil majors. Falling crude = wider crack spreads (prices via Finnhub, as of 11:45 AM ET):

  • MPC +2.5% ($432.74) — a new 52-week high
  • VLO +2.7% ($417.14) — just below its $419.04 52-week high
  • XOM 0.0% ($164.02) and CVX -0.1% ($206.47) — the majors are sitting this one out

Technicals

  • MPC +2.5% — in a clean uptrend, well above the 20-day (~$401) and 50-day (~$365) moving averages; printed a fresh 52-week high of $432.91; support ~$379, resistance is open sky.
  • VLO +2.7% — above the 20-day (~$391) and 50-day (~$355); the $419.04 52-week high sits just overhead; support ~$360.
  • XOM 0.0% — flat, holding above the 20-day (~$162.80) and 50-day (~$160.50); recent range $155.85–$169.64.
  • CVX -0.1% — choppy: below the 20-day (~$208.60) but above the 50-day (~$202.60); bounced off a $202.22 morning dip.

Takeaway

Cheap crude is good news for refiners — today's energy rally is about margins, not barrels.
