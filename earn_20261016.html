A bank-and-insurance morning to close out bank earnings week. All five notable names report today, Friday, October 16, 2026, before the open — no major after-close reporters are on the calendar. Estimates below are consensus figures from MarketBeat, cross-checked against other earnings calendars.

TRV — The Travelers Companies

  • When: Today before the open, Q3 2026
  • Consensus: EPS $7.39, revenue $11.36 billion (MarketBeat)
  • What to watch:
  • Catastrophe losses and the combined ratio — the swing factor for underwriting profit
  • Premium growth and pricing in business and personal insurance
  • Net investment income as a cushion if underwriting softens

TFC — Truist Financial

  • When: Today before the open, Q3 2026
  • Consensus: EPS $1.14, revenue $5.36 billion (MarketBeat)
  • What to watch:
  • Net interest income and net interest margin, after about 3% in recent quarters
  • Loan and deposit growth, and deposit costs
  • Credit quality — charge-offs and provisions — plus efficiency

MTB — M&T Bank

  • When: Today before the open, Q3 2026
  • Consensus: EPS $4.93, revenue $2.53 billion (MarketBeat)
  • What to watch:
  • Net interest margin, which was 3.70% in Q2, and net interest income
  • Fee income after a strong Q2, and loan growth in the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic
  • Credit trends versus full-year charge-off guidance of about 37 basis points of average loans

CFG — Citizens Financial Group

  • When: Today before the open, Q3 2026
  • Consensus: EPS $1.39, revenue $2.34 billion (MarketBeat)
  • What to watch:
  • Net interest income and margin expansion, and deposit costs
  • Loan growth, especially commercial and consumer
  • Credit quality and capital return

RF — Regions Financial

  • When: Today before the open, Q3 2026
  • Consensus: EPS $0.66, revenue $1.99 billion (MarketBeat)
  • What to watch:
  • Net interest margin, around 3.7% in recent reporting, and net interest income
  • Loan growth and deposit mix in the South, Midwest and Texas
  • Credit quality and efficiency, after an efficiency ratio in the mid-50s recently

Sources: MarketBeat earnings pages for TRV, TFC, MTB, CFG and RF for October 16, 2026; TipRanks and other earnings calendars for today's reporter list.
