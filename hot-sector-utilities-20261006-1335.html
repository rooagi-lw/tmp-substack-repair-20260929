Hot Sector: Utilities +2.5%

🔥 Utilities +2.5% — the sector's biggest one-day move in weeks, and it wasn't about interest rates. A landmark Google–Constellation nuclear deal ignited the independent power producers. Data as of ~1:35 PM ET.

Why it's moving

  • ⚛️ Google × Constellation: a 20-year PPA for 890 MW of new nuclear capacity via uprates at 11 Constellation reactors in Illinois, Pennsylvania and New Jersey — $4.3B of Constellation investment, first power 2028 — plus a 15-year, 2,700 MW supply deal in PJM. The WSJ calls it the largest-ever uprate deal between a nuclear operator and a tech company (per Reuters, WSJ, company release).
  • ⚡ Sympathy rally: Talen Energy +13%, Vistra +12%, NRG +9% — the market repriced the whole nuclear-IPP complex (per WSJ midday).
  • 💰 Yield tailwind: the 10-year Treasury slipped 4bp to ~5.26%, off multi-year highs — a bid for rate-sensitive utilities (per IBD).

Driving it

Independent power producers / nuclear — not regulated distribution. CEG +15.2% to ~$308 (6.77% of XLU), VST +12.2% to ~$163; the regulated core (NEE, SO, DUK) rose only ~1% on the rate move (CEG/VST per Finnhub; weight per Indmoney).

Technicals

  • CEG +15.2% — gap-up from $267.62 to ~$308 reclaims the 20-day ($268.70) and 50-day ($271.47) MAs; broke the Sept 8 swing high ~$305.80; support $267–271, resistance $412.70 (52-week high); hourly momentum vertical after three up days; 15-min: opened +8.8% and held near the $309.80 session high (levels per Finnhub candles).
  • VST +12.2% — cleared the 200-day MA ($154.91) for the first time since ~Feb; above the 20-day ($142.35) and 50-day ($143.31); support $150–155, resistance $169.76 (60-day high); hourly: gapped +9% premarket, extended to the $162.69 high; 15-min: steady drive from the $151.47 open (levels per Finnhub candles).
  • NEE +1.0% — regulated core riding the rate move; 52-week range $74.41–$98.75 (per MarketWatch, stockstotrade).
