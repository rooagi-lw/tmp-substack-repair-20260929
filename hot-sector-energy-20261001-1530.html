Energy +1.76% — crude's $100 tug-of-war is handing the tape to refiners and the majors again.

Energy is the session's hottest sector (as of 3:30 PM ET), with XLE driving from a $61.03 open low to a $62.69 session high as refined-products markets tighten faster than crude itself.

Why it's moving

  • ⛽ China halted refined fuel exports for at least the first week of October, tightening an already-squeezed global products market (per DTN's Thursday oil update).
  • 🛢️ Russia extended its diesel export ban through end-October, and the Trump administration is urging EU countries to release emergency diesel stocks — or face a possible US diesel export ban (per Reuters via DTN).
  • 🌍 Brent reclaimed $100 intraday before fading to the high $90s; with US–Iran talks stalled, the geopolitical risk premium stays embedded (per DTN).
  • 📈 A tightening global fuels market is outweighing resurging Middle East crude flows — refiners, not producers, are today's leverage (per DTN).

Driving it: downstream refiners + oil majors

  • VLO +5.0% — refiners earn the crude-to-product spread, and diesel margins are widening on the export squeeze.
  • CVX +1.3% — integrated majors riding both barrels and downstream.
  • XOM +0.5% — steadier, but green with the complex.

Technicals

  • VLO +5.0% — clear uptrend, above the 20-day ($384.55) and 50-day ($348.17) MAs; support $384–386 (today's low + prior close), resistance $419.04 (Sep 21 high); five straight rising closes; today opened flat, dipped to $384.58, then drove to $407.14.
  • CVX +1.3% — choppy between the 20-day ($209.41) and 50-day ($201.76); support $201–203, resistance $217.78 (52-week high); today: open $203.33, session $202.82–207.39.
  • XOM +0.5% — range-bound just above the 20-day ($162.77); support $155–156, resistance $169.64; today: open $161.50, session $160.81–163.99.

Takeaway: When governments start rationing diesel exports, the crack spread becomes the market — refiners are today's real trade.
