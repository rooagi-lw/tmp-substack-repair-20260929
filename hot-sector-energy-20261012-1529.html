Energy +1.3% — crude jumps on Saudi and Hormuz supply attacks, and Big Oil clears buy points

Why it's moving

🛢️ WTI crude +2.2% to $93.83 a barrel and Brent +1.9% to $106.75, according to Investor's Business Daily and Investopedia — crude's highest in nearly two weeks.

💥 Iran-backed Houthi militants attacked Saudi Arabia's King Khalid International Airport over the weekend, killing more than a dozen people and injuring more than 300, per Investor's Business Daily, following attacks there last week and fresh attacks on oil tankers in the Strait of Hormuz.

⚖️ Goldman Sachs raised its December Brent forecast to $90 from $85, citing persistent supply risks, per Investor's Business Daily — even as traders doubted the claimed Russia-Ukraine "energy ceasefire."

Driving it

Integrated majors and Permian producers led, not refiners. COP +2.3% to $137.21, FANG +1.8% to $195.67, XOM +1.7% to $171.87, and CVX +1.5% to $215.20, per Finnhub as of 3:29 PM ET. Refiners PSX, VLO and MPC rose less than 1%, per Investor's Business Daily. Jefferies also raised its FANG price target to $215 from $205 over the weekend, per Investor's Business Daily.

Technicals

XOM +1.7% — daily uptrend, above the 20-day MA ($163.50) and 50-day MA ($161.57); cleared the $170.29 swing high, session high $172.55; hourly uptrend intact, 15-min holding near highs after opening at $171.01.

CVX +1.5% — daily uptrend, above the 20-day MA ($207.79) and 50-day MA ($204.27); resistance $217.78, support $207.79; hourly uptrend, today ranged $213.73–$216.69.

COP +2.3% — daily uptrend, above the 20-day MA ($129.97) and 50-day MA ($129.38); resistance $141.62, support $130; hourly uptrend, today ranged $135.76–$137.47.

FANG +1.8% — daily mixed: above the 20-day MA ($189.84) but still below the 50-day MA ($196.59) after spiking to $198.34; hourly uptrend, 15-min fading back under the 50-day line.

Takeaway: Energy is pricing supply risk, not demand — with crude above $93 and XOM clearing its buy point, this rally holds only as long as Hormuz and Saudi headlines keep a risk premium in oil.
