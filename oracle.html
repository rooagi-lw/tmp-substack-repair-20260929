📌 What happened

  • Bloomberg reported Thursday morning (Sep 24) that Oracle (ORCL) sent a "force majeure" notice to a Blue Owl Capital unit over Project Jupiter — the 1,400-acre, 2.45 GW data-center campus in Doña Ana County, New Mexico, part of the OpenAI/SoftBank/Oracle $500B Stargate plan.

  • Oracle wants the right to delay payments if the campus misses its planned 2028 go-live — "rather than exiting as the main tenant."

  • The stress is real: the campus depends on Bloom Energy gas fuel cells; a key pipeline's commissioning slipped to Feb 1, 2027 after New Mexico's State Land Office rejected its route applications; an $18B project loan from ~20 banks was quoted below face value this month.

  • ORCL slid −6.9% to $134.66 by mid-morning, breaking below its 50-day moving average on heavy volume; the stock is down 31% year-to-date. Blue Owl (OWL) fell ~3.3%. Oracle says the project "remains on our planned schedule."

📊 Why it matters

  • The market's focus is rotating from huge AI orders to delivery schedules and capital recovery. If Oracle is bracing for cost and delay risk on a flagship Stargate site, the whole AI-buildout financing chain looks strained.

  • The contagion is direct and chip-specific: SOXX fell −1.74% while the same AI-accelerator and memory names that had rallied +14% over six sessions (NVDA, AVGO, AMD, ARM, SNDK, MU) all turned red.

  • This is an AI-capex-demand/financing scare, not a fab-cycle problem: equipment and foundry names are quieter; the unwind is concentrated in AI/logic and memory — the segments that led the run-up.

Takeaway: The AI trade's risk has rotated from "will they spend?" to "can they deliver — and get paid back?" Chip stocks are now the collateral.
