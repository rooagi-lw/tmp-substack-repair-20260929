🔥 Homebuilders +1.5% — XHB is the S&P's best sector today, up +1.5% while the S&P 500 adds just +0.2%, as beaten-down builders rebound.

📌 Why it's moving

  • 💰 Berkshire Hathaway bought ~$212M of Lennar (LEN) across Sept 17, 18 and 21 — 2.6–2.74M shares, lifting its Class A stake above 10% (23.7M shares), an ~81% increase since Q2 (Form 4 filed Sept 21; Motley Fool ran the story today). Per Barron's, "investors like the Berkshire purchases."

  • ⚠️ Don't read this as a rates story: the 10-year Treasury sits at 5.17–5.23%, a multi-decade high — yields are a headwind, not a tailwind.

  • 📉 Analysts stay cautious: KBW cut its LEN target to $75 three days ago, Citi cut to $85, and LEN's Q3 EPS $1.23 missed the $1.29 estimate with revenue down 8.7% YoY and the delivery forecast cut.

🏭 Driving it: residential homebuilders, broad-based — all four majors opened green and trade near session highs: PHM +1.3% ($119.74), LEN +0.7% ($82.07), KBH +0.6% ($47.95), DHI +0.6% ($141.13).
