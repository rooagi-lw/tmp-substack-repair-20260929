Earnings Preview: PEP, TLRY, Samsung

The Q3 earnings calendar is still thin, but today has a few names that matter: PepsiCo reports before the open, Tilray Brands goes after the close, and Samsung's record preliminary Q3 numbers already rattled semiconductor stocks overnight.

PepsiCo (PEP) - Before the open

  • Releases Q3 results around 6:00 AM ET; analyst call at 8:15 AM ET.
  • Consensus: about $2.30 EPS and roughly $24.95 billion in revenue, which would be ~4.2% year-over-year revenue growth.
  • What to watch:
  • North American Frito-Lay volumes — whether smaller packs and affordability pushes are reversing share losses to private-label rivals.
  • Pricing power vs. margins as consumers stay value-conscious and costs bite.
  • Full-year guidance: management currently guides 4-6% core constant-currency EPS growth (FY EPS guide of $8.55-$8.71); the stock is down roughly 25% from its year-to-date high, so the bar is low but skepticism is high.

Tilray Brands (TLRY) - After the close

  • Reports fiscal Q1 2027 results after the bell.
  • Consensus: a loss of about $0.16 per share on roughly $268 million in revenue.
  • What to watch:
  • Cannabis segment margins vs. beverage and distribution growth — which side is carrying the quarter.
  • The path to profitability: cash flow and how fast losses are narrowing.
  • Any commentary on U.S. cannabis tax relief, a key overhang for the sector.

Samsung Electronics - Prelim Q3 out overnight

  • Preliminary Q3: operating profit of 107.4 trillion won (about $80 billion) — a record, but just under the 108.7 trillion consensus; revenue of 195 trillion won vs. 199 trillion expected. Full results on October 29.
  • What to watch for U.S. investors:
  • HBM and DRAM pricing — AI-driven memory demand is the story behind the record.
  • Foundry share vs. TSMC, which reported September revenue up 54.6% and Q3 revenue up 50%.
  • How the high bar resets expectations for Micron (MU slipped ~1% overnight) ahead of its own print.
