Why it's moving

📉 The 10-year Treasury yield fell about 13 basis points to 5.05% as of 3:43 ET, its lowest in three weeks — per WSJ, it slipped to 5.062% from 5.189% on Wednesday, even as the data argued the other way.

🔥 September PPI rose 1.3% month over month, more than double the 0.6% consensus per WSJ, and retail sales rose 0.8%, beating expectations — yet yields kept falling as flight-to-safety demand from the AI selloff outweighed inflation fears, according to WSJ.

🏠 Falling yields are oxygen for housing: per Benzinga, rate-sensitive industries led Thursday, with the home-construction ETF ITB up 3.6% while chips lagged again — SOXX -0.6% and XLK -0.7% on the day.

💰 Analysts are split on the leaders: Citigroup raised its D.R. Horton price target to $150 today, per MarketBeat, while RBC cut its target — the bounce is a rates story first, a stock story second.

Driving it

Homebuilders themselves, not housing-adjacent retail, are doing the work — the pure builders are up two to three times the XHB (+3.1%) average:

  • LEN (Lennar) +5.7% to $77.70
  • DHI (D.R. Horton) +4.6% to $139.78
  • NVR +3.6% to $5,937
  • PHM (PulteGroup) +3.5% to $113.14

Technicals

  • LEN +5.7% — daily downtrend intact, still below the 20-day MA near $78.8 and the 50-day near $82.2; support $71.78, today it drove from a $75.21 low to a $78.21 high and is testing that 20-day line into the close.
  • DHI +4.6% — below its 50-day MA at $141.93, but back above the 20-day near $137.4; support $130.40, resistance $141.93–$146; hourly uptrend all session, 15-min holding near the $140.23 high.
  • PHM +3.5% — still below the 20-day near $116.4 and 50-day near $122.6; support $110.54, today $111.32–$113.99 with price pressing the session high.
  • NVR +3.6% — below the 20-day near $6,080 and 50-day near $6,217; support $5,617, today $5,795–$6,045, a steady climb off the open.

Takeaway: This is a relief bounce inside a downtrend — builders are still below their 50-day averages, so the rally lives or dies with the 10-year yield holding near 5.05%.
