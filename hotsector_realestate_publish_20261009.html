Hot Sector: Real Estate +1.6%

Real Estate (XLRE) +1.6% — cell-tower REITs are ripping as SpaceX's $8 billion spectrum deal turns tower landlords into the market's hottest trade.

Why it's moving

  • 🛰️ SpaceX–Grain spectrum deal: SpaceX agreed to buy Grain Management's nationwide 800 MHz portfolio — announced October 8, about $8 billion in cash per the Wall Street Journal, pending FCC approval — to build Starlink Mobile into "a major mobile carrier in the U.S."
  • 📡 New-customer thesis: Bernstein says the deal keeps "the terrestrial network build option very much alive"; Morgan Stanley sees new leasing opportunities for tower operators.
  • 📉 Carrier pain, tower gain: T-Mobile fell 12% while AT&T and Verizon each dropped 10%, per Barron's, as investors priced in a fourth national carrier — and tower REITs own the poles a new network would need.
  • 💵 Oversold bounce: Mizuho flagged rate-battered tower REITs like American Tower as oversold — the 10-year yield sits near multidecade highs around 5.28%, per Realtor.com via Fox Business.

Driving it

Towers — the wireless-infrastructure slice of real estate: CCI +12.2% to $77.30, AMT +7.4% to $179.02, SBAC +6.1% to $180.45. The rest of the REIT complex was flat to modestly higher — this is a tower story, not a rates story.

Technicals (as of 1:33 PM ET)

  • CCI +12.2% — blasted through both the 20-day ($70.16) and 50-day ($73.31) moving averages; resistance at the 3-month high $79.47 (session high $79.42); hourly uptrend +17% over the last 5 sessions; opened $77.08 and held gains into the afternoon.
  • AMT +7.4% — new 3-month intraday high ($181.30); above the 20-day ($169.95) and 50-day ($172.50); hourly uptrend +10.7% over 5 sessions; opened $179.00 and never looked back.
  • SBAC +6.1% — above the 20-day ($170.81) and 50-day ($179.22); resistance at the recent swing high $185.47 (session high $185.00); hourly uptrend +13.1% over 5 sessions.

📌 Takeaway

SpaceX just turned cell towers from rate victims into growth assets — today's move prices in a fourth national carrier before a single tower is leased.
