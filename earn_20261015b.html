Four of today's five widely followed reporters are already out — and all four beat on earnings. Interactive Brokers closes the day after the bell. Here are the numbers.

TSM — Taiwan Semiconductor (reported before the open)

  • Result: Revenue of NT$1.494 trillion (about $46.7 billion) versus $45.67 billion expected (FactSet, via Investor's Business Daily); net profit of NT$763.1 billion, up 68.7% year over year; earnings of $4.60 per ADR versus $4.46 expected.
  • Margins: Gross margin of 66.4%, inside the company's 65%–67% guided range; operating margin of 58.7%. Advanced nodes (7nm and below) were 79% of wafer revenue, and high-performance computing was 68% of revenue.
  • Guidance: Fourth-quarter revenue guided to $52.3–54.3 billion, with the 2026 capital budget reiterated at $60–64 billion — the AI demand signal the whole chip sector trades on.
  • Reaction: The ADR still fell about 2% in premarket trading, as hot September CPI and jumping Treasury yields weighed on chip multiples despite the beat (TradingKey, CNBC).

SCHW — Charles Schwab (reported before the open)

  • Result: Adjusted EPS of $1.69 versus $1.66 expected (Zacks); GAAP EPS of $1.62. Revenue of $7.17 billion versus $7.19 billion expected — in line, and up 16.9% year over year (company release via Business Wire).
  • Growth: Core net new assets of $190.4 billion, a record, up 38% year over year, with 1.2 million new brokerage accounts opened in the quarter.
  • Watch now: Whether client cash sorting keeps reversing and net interest income holds its momentum into Q4.

PNC — PNC Financial (reported before the open)

  • Result: Adjusted EPS of $5.09, ahead of the $4.89–$4.98 consensus range; GAAP EPS of $4.97. Revenue of $6.56 billion versus $6.59 billion expected (AlphaStreet) — a slight miss, up 11% year over year (Dow Jones via Morningstar).
  • Drivers: Net interest income rose 15% to $4.21 billion on commercial loan growth, the FirstBank acquisition and lower funding costs; total loans ended the quarter up 16% at $378.8 billion. Profit absorbed $36 million of FirstBank integration costs.
  • Watch now: Credit quality — provisions rose to $189 million — as loan growth continues.

PLD — Prologis (reported before the open)

  • Result: EPS of $1.60 versus $0.78 expected (MarketBeat) — a wide beat for the warehouse REIT.
  • Guidance: Full-year 2026 guidance updated to $6.25–$6.29 per share.
  • Reaction: Shares were down about 2.3% despite the beat, caught in the same rate-driven selloff hitting real estate.
  • Watch now: Occupancy and rent growth for the cleanest read on warehouse demand, plus progress on the data center development pipeline.

IBKR — Interactive Brokers (reports after the close)

  • Consensus: EPS of $0.67 on revenue of $1.86 billion (AlphaStreet and Seeking Alpha consensus; MarketBeat: $0.668 on $1.86 billion).
  • What to watch: Trading volumes (DARTs) — whether the activity surge that drove a record Q2 held through September.
  • What to watch: Net interest income on client cash and margin loans as rates shift.
  • What to watch: New account growth and total client equity.
