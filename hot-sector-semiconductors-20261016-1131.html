Hot Sector: Semiconductors -2.1%

Semiconductors -2.1% — the chip rout stretched to an eighth session as AI-financing fears, not demand, did the damage. Data as of 11:31 ET.

Why it's moving

  • 💰 CoreWeave (CRWV) down more than 7%, leading Nasdaq 100 losers per Barchart, as swaps priced a more than 50% chance it defaults on its debt within five years; fellow neocloud Nebius fell more than 5%
  • 📉 The broader chips index lost 1.8% mid-morning and is set for its steepest 8-session percentage decline since the April 2025 "Liberation Day" selloff, according to Reuters
  • 🧠 Reuters: investors now question whether data-centre operators can turn profitable in time to repay debts as borrowing costs rise — and some Anthropic investors are weighing a ~$1.5 trillion IPO valuation, down from $2 trillion expected earlier
  • 💾 Memory led the slide: Micron (MU) -3.5%, with Seagate and Western Digital down more than 4% per Barchart, extending a rout that began on fears of Chinese memory competition
  • 🏦 Money rotated, it did not leave: Financials (XLF) +1.0%, Materials (XLB) +1.3% and Energy (XLE) +1.2% all rose while chips fell

Driving it

Memory chips and AI-infrastructure names drove it, with equipment also lower (Lam Research and KLA down more than 2% per Barchart). Intel (INTC) -3.9%, Micron (MU) -3.5%, AMD -3.5%, Marvell (MRVL) -3.0% and Broadcom (AVGO) -2.6%. The outlier: Nvidia (NVDA) -0.3%, holding up as CEO Jensen Huang reiterated customers are placing AI chip orders almost daily.

Technicals

  • INTC -3.9% at $91.67 — below its 20-day ($114) and 50-day ($103) averages; support $91.00 (today's low), resistance $95.37 (prior close); hourly downtrend, 15-min still pressing session lows
  • MU -3.5% at $898.54 — below its 20-day ($1,042) and 50-day ($982) averages; support $890 (today's low), resistance $931 (prior close); hourly down, 15-min bounce off $890 stalled under the $908 session high
  • AMD -3.5% at $542.54 — below its 20-day ($611), just above its 50-day ($535); support $539 (today's low), resistance $551 (today's high); hourly down, 15-min basing above $539
  • MRVL -3.0% at $234.07 — below its 20-day ($264) and 50-day ($240) averages; support $231 (today's low), resistance $241 (prior close); hourly down, 15-min off the lows but still below the open

Takeaway: This is a financing scare, not a demand scare — TSMC and ASML just beat, yet chips fall because the market no longer trusts AI debt to pay for itself.
